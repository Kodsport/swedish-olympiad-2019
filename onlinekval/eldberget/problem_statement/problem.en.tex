\problemname{Eldberget}
During an excursion to the fire mountains \emph{Yanar Dag} in Azerbaijan (host country for this year's International Olympiad in Informatics), you have gotten lost!
The mountains have the shape of a grid with $R$ rows and $C$ columns.
You are standing in the top-left corner of the grid and want to get to the excursion bus which is in the bottom-right corner.
Since the bus is leaving soon, you want to get there as quickly as possible.
To reach the bus, you can move to a cell directly above, to the right, below, or to the left of the one you are standing on.

On the fire mountains there are however a number of flames, caused by natural gas seeping out from the mountains.
Since you are wearing very nice clothes, you do not want to run through more flames than necessary.
More specifically, you are willing to pass through at most $K$ flames on your way to the bus.

Your task is to compute how quickly you can reach the bus if you may pass through at most $K$ flames.

\section*{Input}
The first line contains three integers $R, C, K$ ($2 \le R, C \le 100$, $0 \le K \le 200$), the number of rows and columns in the grid that the fire mountains consist of, and the number of flames you can run through.

The following $R$ lines constitute a description of the fire mountains.
The $i$th of these lines contains $C$ characters describing what the $i$th row looks like.
Each character is either a dot (\texttt{.}) if the cell is empty or a hash (\texttt{\#}) if the cell contains a flame. The top-left cell and the bottom-right cell are always dots.

\section*{Output}
Print a single integer $N$ -- the minimum number of steps you need to reach the bus.
If you cannot reach the destination without passing through more than $K$ flames, print ``\texttt{nej}'' instead.

\section*{Scoring}
Your solution will be tested on a set of test groups.
To earn points for a group, you must pass all test cases in that group.
