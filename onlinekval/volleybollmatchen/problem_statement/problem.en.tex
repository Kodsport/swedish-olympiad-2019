\problemname{Volleybollmatchen}
You have been hired by PO-Volley (the Made-Up Organization for Volleyball)
to keep track of the score in a volleyball match between the two teams the Algorithmics and the Beaver Scouts.

In volleyball you play best of $3$ sets, that is, the team
that first wins $2$ sets wins the match. In the first two sets, you play to
$25$ points, and if there is a third set, you play to $15$.
You must win by $2$ points, so it is the team that first has
at least $25$ or $15$ points respectively, and also has $2$ more points than the opponent,
that wins the set. Given which team wins each ball, print the result of the match.

\section*{Input}
The first line contains an integer $N$ ($1 \le N \le 200$).
Then follows a string of $N$ characters consisting of ``\texttt{A}'' and ``\texttt{B}'', describing a complete match.
The $i$th character is ``\texttt{A}'' if the Algorithmics win the $i$th ball, and ``\texttt{B}'' if the Beaver Scouts win the $i$th ball.

The match will be complete, i.e.\ one of the teams will have reached $2$ set wins at the end, and no extra balls will have been played.

\section*{Output}
Print a line with two integers. The first number should be the number of sets the Algorithmics won
and the second number should be the number of sets the Beaver Scouts won.

\section*{Scoring}
Your solution will be tested on a set of test groups.
To earn points for a group, you must pass all test cases in that group.

\noindent
\begin{tabular}{| l | l | l |}
\hline
Group & Points & Constraints \\ \hline
$1$    & $42$          &  All sets will be won at exactly $25$, $25$, and $15$ points respectively, that is, it will never become $24$--$24$ or $14$--$14$\\ \hline
$2$    & $58$          &  No additional constraints \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

\section*{Explanation of Samples}
In the first sample, team A wins the first set $25$--$0$,
team B wins the second set $0$--$25$, and team A wins the deciding
third set $15$--$0$.
The result of the match is thus $2$--$1$.

In the second sample, team B wins both the first and second sets $25$--$27$, and the result of the match is thus $0$--$2$.

Note that only the first sample could occur in test group $1$.
