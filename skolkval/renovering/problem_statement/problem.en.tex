\problemname{Renoveringen}
\noindent
Johanna is renovating her apartment.
Since Johanna does not like to leave things to chance, she has planned
in detail exactly how many nails she needs during the renovation.
In total, she needs $N$ nails with lengths $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_N$.
In her nail box, she has $M$ nails of lengths $y_1, y_2, \dots, y_M$.

If Johanna needs a nail with length $x_i$, she can use a nail with
length $y_j$ if $x_i \le y_j$, since she can cut the longer nail
until it is exactly the right length.
However, she cannot combine two short nails into a longer nail,
or cut a nail multiple times (it only has one nail head, after all).

Before Johanna starts the renovation, she wants to know:
\begin{itemize}
    \item how many nails she needs to buy, and
    \item what lengths the nails she needs to buy should have.
\end{itemize}

She wants to buy as few nails as possible, and also wants
to buy nails of as short total length as possible.

\section*{Input}
The first line contains two integers $1 \le N \le 15$ and $1 \le M \le 15$ -- the number of nails Johanna needs and the number of nails Johanna has.
The second line contains $N$ integers $1 \le x_1, x_2, \dots, x_N \le 100$, the lengths of the nails Johanna needs.
The third line contains $M$ integers $1 \le y_1, y_2, \dots, y_M \le 100$, the lengths of the nails Johanna has.

\section*{Output}
The program should first print a single integer: the minimum number of nails Johanna needs to buy.
On the next line, the program should print the lengths of the nails Johanna should buy, in increasing order.

\section*{Scoring}
\noindent
\begin{tabular}{| l | l | p{12cm} |}
  \hline
  \textbf{Group} & \textbf{Points} & \textbf{Constraints} \\ \hline
  $1$    & $40$       & Johanna needs to use all nails she already has,
  and she does not need to cut any of them. \\ \hline
  $2$    & $60$       & No additional constraints. \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

\section*{Explanation of Samples}
In sample 1, Johanna only needs to supplement with three extra nails of lengths $13$, $28$, and $77$.

In sample 2, Johanna needs to buy one more nail of length $11$, and also cut a nail of length $100$ to $50$.
She could have bought a nail of length $50$ and cut the nail of length $100$ to length $11$,
but then she would need to buy nails of greater total length.
